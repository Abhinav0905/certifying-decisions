independent experimental tasks.

Material limitations and warnings
- The experiment tests affine action preference. It contains no action
  feasibility constraints, reservation protocol, authorization system or
  atomic commit implementation.
- Reported latency is simulated cumulative tool delay. Policy computation,
  language-model execution and intermediate planning time are not charged.
  The final execution gap is fixed at 0.5 simulated time units.
- Source observations are sampled at read completion. This does not establish
  correctness for a tool whose result was sampled earlier but timestamped at
  receipt. Such a tool needs its real source timestamp in the model.
- All gated methods use the same current-time certificate and deadline. The
  static_h2 ablation freezes ages only in its forecast, not in its final gate.
- At most one read per source is permitted. Claims about coverage apply to
  that restricted query model. The study assumes serial calls.
- full_order optimizes the final predicted margin over complete permutations
  of all five reads, then stops early on the common gate and skips infeasible
  reads. It is not an optimal refresh-subset policy or an online oracle. In the
  independent_option family, the fifth source has a common coefficient that
  cancels in every action comparison; full refresh still includes it.
- Bound-misspecification conditions reuse latent random draws but regenerate
  historical observations and the target offset. They are not identical fixed
  decision instances with only a changed physical rate.
- The initial margin is deliberately controlled by construction. The generated
  episodes do not estimate production error frequencies.
- Drift bounds are known inputs, not empirically calibrated guarantees. Bound
  violations are a deliberate stress condition and can invalidate certificates.
- The affine certificate allows numerical margin tolerance 1e-10; an executed
  action is counted invalid above regret 1e-8. Ideal-arithmetic statements and
  numerical conventions should be distinguished.
- Confidence intervals are unadjusted across 360 comparisons. The report does
  not claim familywise statistical significance across all conditions/slices.
